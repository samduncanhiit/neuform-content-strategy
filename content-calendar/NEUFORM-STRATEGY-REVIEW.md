- **Avg likes/post:** 3,859
- **Avg comments/post:** 111
- **Avg engagement rate:** 0.32%
- **Industry benchmark:** 1.5-3.5% for fitness creators with 1M+ followers

### The Problem
**Chontel's engagement rate is critically low at 0.32%.** For context:
- Kayla Itsines (16M followers): ~0.8% ER
- Krissy Cela (3.5M): ~1.8% ER
- Hanna Oberg (2.3M): ~1.4% ER

With 1.2M followers, Chontel should be averaging **15,000-40,000 likes per post**, not 3,800. This means ~97% of her audience is inactive or the algorithm isn't showing her content.

1. **"The feminine urge to just lift heavy" — 6,345 likes, 0.53% ER** — Relatable opinion, invites discussion, personality-driven
2. **Magazine cover posts — 5,600-20,800 likes** — Social proof / authority

### What's NOT Working (Bottom Posts)
1. **"Download @neuform_ app" — 720 likes, 0.06% ER** — Direct promotional = algorithm death
2. **Charity ambassador post — 893 likes** — Off-brand, not what followers signed up for
3. **Generic motivational quotes — 846 likes** — Too polished, not personal, feels like a template

### Root Cause Analysis
Applying the **Theory of Constraints** (marketing-psychology skill):

The bottleneck is NOT content quality — Chontel's production is good. The bottleneck is:

1. **Captions are too "polished corporate"** — reads like copywriting, not like a real person talking. Her recent captions use poetic formatting ("not the hard days / but the unclear ones") that feels inauthentic for a mum-of-5 gym owner from Brisbane.

2. **No hooks** — Most posts start with a soft statement, not a pattern interrupt. The algorithm decides in 0.5 seconds whether to show your content. Soft openings = buried.

3. **Direct promotional posts kill reach** — "Download @neuform_ app" got her WORST engagement. Instagram punishes overt ads by suppressing distribution.

4. **No conversation starters** — Low comment counts (8-20) mean the algorithm treats her posts as low-engagement. Comments are the #1 signal for distribution.

5. **Hashtag strategy is nonexistent** — Only 1 hashtag used across 12 recent posts (#weareNeuform). This kills discoverability.

6. **Bio link goes to Linktree, not Neuform** — Friction in the conversion path. Every extra click loses ~50% of traffic.

## Strategy Revisions Required

### 1. CAPTION VOICE — Fix Immediately

**Current voice (from her posts):**
> "I think this is the part people struggle with the most… not the hard days but the unclear ones where nothing feels certain"

**Problem:** This reads like a LinkedIn motivational post, not a mum from Capalaba who's just trained at 5am with Vegemite on her shirt.

**Revised voice guide (applying copywriting skill — "Customer Language Over Company Language"):**
- Write like she's texting her best friend
- Use Australian slang naturally (not forced)
- Short sentences. Punchy. Real.
- Reference her actual life — the kids' names, HIIT Capalaba, specific moments
- Imperfect grammar is FINE — it signals authenticity
- Never use poetic line breaks unless it's genuinely emotional content

**EVERY caption in the calendar needs to be reviewed against this. The current drafts are too polished.**

### 2. HOOK STRATEGY — Missing from Current Captions

Applying the **Reel Script template** (social-content skill):
```
Hook (0-2 sec): [Pattern interrupt or bold claim]
Setup (2-5 sec): [Context for the tip]
Value (5-25 sec): [The actual advice/content]
CTA (25-30 sec): [Follow, comment, share, link]
```

**Current Chontel posts have NO hooks.** Her recent captions start with:
- "I think this is the part people struggle with..." (weak)
- "There's no end goal for me..." (about her, not viewer)
- "Studies show morning exercisers..." (textbook, not scroll-stopping)

**Every Reel/TikTok must open with a hook that creates a curiosity gap in under 2 seconds.**

### 3. CONVERSION STRATEGY — Neuform CTA is Wrong

**Current approach:** "Download @neuform_ app 📲" → 720 likes (WORST performing post)

**Why it fails (applying Zero-Price Effect + Endowment Effect from marketing-psychology):**
- Direct "download my app" is a PUSH. People resist being pushed.
- The free trial isn't emphasised. "Free" triggers irrational preference (Zero-Price Effect).
- No demonstration of value before asking for action.

**Revised conversion framework:**
1. **Never post "download my app" as standalone content.** Ever.
2. **Show the result, then mention the tool.** "She followed my 8-week program → here's her result → that program is on Neuform"
3. **Lead with the free trial.** "7 days free, no commitment" should appear in EVERY bio, EVERY Story CTA, EVERY pinned comment.
4. **Use the Endowment Effect:** Once someone uses the app for 7 days, they "own" their progress data, workout history, and meal plans. They won't want to lose it.
5. **DM automation** ("Comment PROGRAM") converts at 5-10x the rate of "link in bio."

### 4. COMMUNITY BUILDING — Currently Nonexistent

**Problem:** Chontel's posts average 14 comments. That's a monologue, not a community.

**Applying social-content skill Engagement Strategy:**

Every post needs ONE of these comment-driving mechanics:
- **Question:** "Which exercise is your favourite? Drop it below"
- **Challenge:** "Tag a mum who needs this"
- **Opinion:** "Agree or disagree?"
- **Fill in the blank:** "My pre-workout is _____"
- **This or that:** "Upper body or lower body today?"

**Rule: No post goes live without a comment-driving CTA at the end.**

**Required:** Daily Reel + daily TikTok + 5-10 Stories minimum

The calendar already plans for this cadence. The issue is Chontel may not be executing at this volume currently. Batch filming days (per social-content skill batching strategy) are essential.

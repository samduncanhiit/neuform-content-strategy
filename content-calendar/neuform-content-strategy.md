## Platform Strategy

### Instagram (Primary — 1.2M followers)
- **Posting cadence:** 1 Reel + 1 Carousel/Static per day, 5-10 Stories daily
- **Best times:** 6-7am AEST (pre-gym crowd), 12pm, 7-8pm
- **Priority formats:** Reels (2x reach of static), Carousels (high saves), Stories (daily connection)
- **CTA placement:** Link in bio always points to Neuform download, mention app naturally in captions
- **Algorithm play:** Reels for reach, Carousels for saves, Stories for loyalty

### TikTok (Growth platform — 49.5K followers, huge upside)
- **Posting cadence:** 1-2 videos per day
- **Best times:** 7-9am AEST, 12-3pm, 7-9pm
- **Priority formats:** Raw/unpolished gym clips, trending sounds, POV content, educational hooks
- **Hashtags:** #gymtok #musclemummy #fitmum #workoutmotivation #neuform #fyp
- **Growth play:** TikTok is where Chontel has the most growth potential (49.5K → 200K+ target). Lean into authenticity and trending formats.

## Hook Formulas (Proven for Fitness)

### Curiosity Hooks
- "I stopped doing [X] and here's what happened to my body"
- "The exercise most people do wrong at the gym"
- "Why I train differently after 5 pregnancies"

### Story Hooks
- "5 babies in 6 years. Here's how I got my strength back."
- "I almost gave up training after my 4th baby"
- "3 years ago I couldn't do a pull-up. Today..."

### Value Hooks
- "3 exercises every mum should be doing"
- "My exact meal prep for the week (takes 1 hour)"
- "The workout split that changed everything for me"

### Contrarian Hooks
- "Stop doing cardio to lose weight. Do this instead."
- "You don't need to train every day to see results"
- "Eating more helped me lose fat — here's how"
